Your candidates complete voice and coding screens on their own time, and AceInt delivers detailed scores, transcripts, and recordings to your team.

## How it works

1. **Create a job role** — Define the role you are hiring for, select the interview type (voice, coding, or behavioral), and configure the question bank and scoring rubric.
2. **Invite candidates** — Generate a shareable invite link or sync candidates directly from your ATS. Candidates join on any device without creating an account.
3. **AI conducts the interview** — AceInt's AI interviewer runs the session autonomously — asking questions, listening to answers, probing for depth, and scoring responses against your rubric.
4. **Review results** — Receive a complete report with scores, transcripts, audio recordings, and AI-generated insights. Share results with your hiring team.
