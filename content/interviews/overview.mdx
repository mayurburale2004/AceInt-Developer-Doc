AceInt AI interviews replace manual phone screens with an autonomous voice interviewer that speaks with candidates in real time, evaluates their responses, and delivers structured scores — without a human on the call. Every interview is asynchronous by default, so your team reviews results on their own schedule rather than coordinating calendars.

The AI interviewer uses speech-to-text (STT) to transcribe candidate answers, a large language model (LLM) to evaluate responses and generate follow-up questions, and text-to-speech (TTS) to deliver questions in a natural voice. Candidates join through a shareable link in any modern browser — no app download or AceInt account required.

## How an AI interview works

When you create an interview, you select a role, choose an interview type, attach a question bank, and configure a scoring rubric. AceInt generates a shareable invite link. The candidate clicks the link, grants microphone access, and the AI interviewer introduces itself, then asks questions one at a time. Follow-up questions are generated automatically based on the candidate's answers. When the session ends, AceInt processes the transcript, scores each response against your rubric, and makes the full report available in your dashboard.

## Use cases

**High-volume screening** — Send the same invite link to hundreds of applicants and receive a ranked, scored list of candidates without scheduling a single call. Because every candidate is evaluated against the same rubric, comparisons are objective and consistent.

**Asynchronous evaluation** — Candidates complete interviews on their own schedule, including evenings and weekends, which increases completion rates for roles with a global applicant pool or tight timelines.

**Structured technical screening** — Technical questions with configurable follow-up depth probe beyond surface-level answers, giving you signal on problem-solving approach and depth of knowledge.

**Behavioral competency assessment** — STAR-format questions surface evidence of past behavior across dimensions like communication, adaptability, and leadership, replacing unstructured recruiter calls.

## Key capabilities

- **AI voice options** — Choose from multiple voices, languages, and speaking paces to match your brand and candidate audience.
- **Question banks** — Build curated sets of technical and behavioral questions per role, or start from a template.
- **Scoring rubrics** — Define evaluation dimensions, set weights, and configure passing thresholds.
- **No-account candidate flow** — Candidates join via link; no sign-up friction.
- **Transcript, scores, and recording** — Every session produces a full turn-by-turn transcript, per-question scores with AI reasoning, and an audio recording.
