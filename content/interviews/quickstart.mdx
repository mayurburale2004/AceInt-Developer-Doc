This guide walks you through every step required to create an AI interview and send the invite link to a candidate. By the end, you will have a fully configured interview session ready to collect responses, and you will know where to find the scored results once the candidate finishes.

1. **Open Interviews** — in the AceInt dashboard, click **Interviews** in the left navigation. This is your central hub for all past and active interview sessions.
2. **Create a New Interview** — click **New Interview** in the top-right corner. A setup wizard opens and guides you through the remaining steps.
3. **Select a Role** — type or search for the role you are hiring for (for example, _Senior Backend Engineer_ or _Customer Success Manager_). Selecting a role loads any previously saved question banks and rubrics associated with that role, so you can reuse configuration across interview cycles.

   <Callout title="Tip">
   If this is your first interview, select a role and AceInt will suggest a starter question bank and default rubric for that role type.
   </Callout>

4. **Choose an Interview Type** — select one of the three interview types:

   - **Technical Screen** — focuses on role-specific technical questions with AI-generated follow-ups.
   - **Behavioral Screen** — uses STAR-format competency questions.
   - **Full Interview** — combines both types in a single session.

   See [Interview Types](/interviews/types) for a full comparison.

5. **Select a Question Bank** — choose an existing question bank from the dropdown or click **Create New** to build one from scratch. The question bank determines which questions the AI asks during the session.

   See [Configure Question Bank](/interviews/configure-question-bank) for details on building and editing question banks.

6. **Set a Scoring Rubric** — choose a rubric or accept the default. The rubric defines the evaluation dimensions (for example, _Technical Accuracy_, _Communication Clarity_) and their weights. You can also configure a passing threshold here.

   See [Set Up Scoring Rubrics](/interviews/configure-scoring) for details.

7. **Configure Voice Settings** — select the AI voice, language, and speaking pace for this interview. Click **Preview** to hear a sample before confirming.

   See [Configure AI Voice](/interviews/configure-voice-settings) for all available options.

8. **Generate the Invite Link** — click **Generate Link**. AceInt creates a unique invite URL for this interview session. Choose between a **single-use** link (one candidate) or a **multi-use** link (multiple candidates, each session tracked separately).
9. **Send the Link to Your Candidate** — copy the invite link and send it via email, ATS, or any messaging tool. You can also use AceInt's built-in email sender by entering the candidate's email address and clicking **Send Invite**.

   <Callout title="Note">
   Candidates do not need an AceInt account to take the interview. They click the link, grant microphone access, and begin immediately.
   </Callout>

10. **Review Results** — once the candidate completes the interview, AceInt sends you an email notification and the session appears in your **Interviews** dashboard with a status of **Ready for Review**. Click the session to access the transcript, scores, AI summary, and audio recording.

    See [Understanding Score Reports](/interviews/results-scores) and [Review Transcripts](/interviews/results-transcripts) for details.

## What happens next

After your first interview, explore the configuration options to refine question banks, adjust rubric weights, and set a passing threshold that matches your hiring bar. You can also duplicate a successful interview setup to reuse it for future candidates in the same role.
