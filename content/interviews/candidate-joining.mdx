Candidates join AceInt AI interviews through a standard browser link — no app download, plugin, or account creation required. The joining experience is designed to be simple and low-friction, but candidates do need a working microphone and a supported browser to participate.

## Supported devices and browsers

AceInt AI interviews run entirely in the browser on both desktop and mobile devices.

| Platform | Supported Browsers |
| --- | --- |
| Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux) | Chrome 90+, Edge 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 15+ |
| Mobile (iOS, Android) | Safari on iOS 15+, Chrome on Android 90+ |

<Callout title="Note">
Desktop is the recommended environment for the best audio quality and the most stable microphone connection. Mobile is fully supported but may be affected by background noise and Bluetooth headset compatibility.
</Callout>

## Joining via the invite link

1. **Open the Link** — click or tap the invite link from the email or message received. The pre-interview landing page loads in the browser, showing the company logo, welcome message, and a **Begin Interview** button.
2. **Read the Pre-Interview Info** — the landing page displays the expected interview length, format (voice interview with an AI), and any instructions from the hiring team. Read it carefully before proceeding.
3. **Click Begin Interview** — click **Begin Interview** to proceed to the microphone permission and audio check step.

## Microphone permission

After clicking **Begin Interview**, the browser displays a permission prompt asking the candidate to allow microphone access. The candidate must click **Allow** (or the equivalent in their browser) to continue.

If the candidate dismisses or blocks the permission prompt, a help screen appears with instructions for re-enabling microphone access in their browser settings.

<Callout title="Tip">
Advise candidates to use headphones or earbuds during the interview. This eliminates echo from the speaker and significantly improves transcription accuracy, which leads to better scoring.
</Callout>

## Microphone and audio check

Before the interview begins, the candidate completes a short audio check:

1. The AI plays a test tone. The candidate confirms they can hear it.
2. The candidate speaks a test phrase. A waveform confirms the microphone is picking up audio.
3. If both checks pass, the candidate clicks **I'm Ready** to start the interview.

If the audio check fails, the candidate sees specific troubleshooting guidance for their detected issue.

## AI introduction

Once the audio check is complete, the AI interviewer introduces itself by name, explains the format of the interview (number of questions, follow-up style, time limits if applicable), and invites the candidate to ask any clarifying questions before the first question begins.

After the introduction, the AI states that it is ready to begin and asks the first question.

## Troubleshooting tips for candidates

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The browser isn't asking for microphone permission">
    Check that the site is not already blocked. In Chrome, click the lock icon in the address bar → **Site settings** → set Microphone to **Allow**, then refresh the page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The audio check waveform isn't moving">
    Make sure no other application (video call, recording software) is using the microphone exclusively. Close other apps and refresh the page. On macOS, check **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone** to confirm the browser has permission.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AI voice is cutting out or hard to hear">
    Check your device volume and try a different browser. On mobile, remove Bluetooth headphones and use wired earbuds or the device speaker, then re-run the audio check.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The link shows an error when I open it">
    The invite link may be expired or have already been used (if it was a single-use link). Contact the hiring team to request a new link.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
