AceInt generates a structured score report for every completed AI interview session. The report is designed to give hiring teams actionable, explainable signal — not just a number, but the AI's reasoning behind each score, broken down by question and evaluation dimension.

Score reports are available in your dashboard as soon as processing completes, typically within 5 to 10 minutes of the session ending.

## Overall score

The overall score is a single percentage (0–100) representing the candidate's weighted average performance across all questions and dimensions in the rubric. It appears at the top of the score report alongside:

- **Pass / Review / Fail label** — determined by the passing threshold configured in your rubric. See [Scoring Rubrics](/interviews/configure-scoring) for threshold configuration.
- **AI summary** — a two to four sentence natural-language summary of the candidate's overall performance, generated by the LLM at the end of scoring.
- **Session metadata** — role, interview type, date, duration, and AI interviewer voice used.

## Pass/Fail threshold

When a passing threshold is set on the rubric:

- **Pass** — overall score ≥ threshold. Candidate meets or exceeds your defined bar.
- **Review** — overall score is within 10 percentage points below the threshold. Candidate is borderline and warrants human review.
- **Fail** — overall score is more than 10 points below the threshold.

If no threshold is set, the label column shows a raw score only.

## Per-dimension breakdown

Below the overall score, the report displays a breakdown by rubric dimension. Each dimension shows:

- The dimension name and description.
- The dimension score (0–100).
- The dimension weight used in calculating the overall score.
- The weighted contribution to the overall score.

This breakdown tells you _where_ a candidate is strong or weak. A candidate with a high Technical Accuracy score but low Communication Clarity score may be a strong individual contributor but a poor fit for a client-facing role.

## Per-question scores and AI reasoning

Expand any question in the **Questions** section to see:

| Field | Type | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `question_text` | string | The exact question the AI asked, including any follow-up questions triggered during the session. |
| `candidate_answer` | string | The transcribed candidate answer. Click **View in Transcript** to jump to that turn in the full transcript. |
| `dimension_scores` | object | Score (0–100) for each applicable dimension, with a one- to two-sentence AI reasoning note explaining why that score was assigned. For example: _"The candidate correctly identified the trade-offs between consistency and availability in distributed systems, but did not address how they would handle network partition scenarios specifically."_ |
| `confidence` | string | Transcription confidence for this answer: **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**. Low confidence indicates audio quality issues that may have affected scoring accuracy. |

## Score confidence

Each per-question score carries a confidence indicator. Low-confidence scores are flagged with a warning icon in the report. Treat low-confidence scores as advisory rather than definitive — listen to the corresponding recording segment to verify the transcription before making a hiring decision based on that score.

<Callout title="Warning">
Do not use AI scores as the sole basis for a hiring decision. Scores are one signal among many. Low-confidence scores, in particular, should always be verified against the recording.
</Callout>

## Comparing scores across candidates

In the **Interviews** list view, click the **Compare** checkbox next to two or more sessions for the same role to open a side-by-side comparison. The comparison view shows:

- Overall scores and Pass/Fail labels.
- Per-dimension scores as a bar chart.
- AI summaries side by side.

Use this view to quickly rank and differentiate candidates before deciding who to advance.

## Sharing scores

Share a score report with a colleague or hiring manager without requiring them to have an AceInt account:

1. Open the score report.
2. Click **Share Report** in the top-right corner.
3. Copy the shareable link or enter an email address to send directly.
4. Set a link expiry (optional) — the default is 30 days.

<Callout title="Note">
Shared score report links are view-only. Recipients cannot edit rubrics, trigger re-scoring, or access any other session in your account.
</Callout>
