Voice settings control how the AI interviewer sounds to candidates. The right configuration makes the interview feel professional and accessible — particularly important when interviewing candidates across different regions or language backgrounds.

You can set voice options at the individual interview level or establish an account-wide default that applies to all new interviews unless overridden.

## Available voices

AceInt provides a library of AI voices across multiple styles and accents. Each voice has a name, a gender presentation label, and a sample clip you can preview before selecting.

To browse voices:

1. Navigate to **Interviews → Configure → Voice Settings**, or open voice settings within the interview setup wizard.
2. Click any voice card to hear a 10-second preview.
3. Select the voice you want to use.

<Callout title="Tip">
Choose a voice that feels neutral and professional for your candidate audience. For roles with a global applicant pool, prefer voices with a neutral accent and a moderate speaking pace.
</Callout>

## Language selection

Select the language the AI uses to deliver questions and respond to candidate prompts. The selected language must match the language of your question bank — mixing languages produces poor transcription quality and scoring accuracy.

<Callout title="Note">
Language selection affects the AI's speech output only. Transcription uses the selected language model automatically. If your candidates speak a language not yet listed, contact AceInt support to request support for additional languages.
</Callout>

## Speaking pace

Control how quickly the AI delivers questions. Three pace options are available:

| Pace | Description | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slow | Deliberate delivery with longer pauses between sentences | Non-native speakers, roles requiring careful comprehension |
| Normal | Conversational pace similar to a human interviewer | Most roles and candidate profiles |
| Fast | Brisk delivery, minimal pauses | Experienced candidates, roles where verbal fluency is assessed |

<Callout title="Warning">
Setting pace to **Fast** for non-native speakers may result in candidates asking for repetition frequently, which extends session length and disrupts the interview flow.
</Callout>

## Previewing voice settings

Before launching an interview, use the **Preview** button in the voice settings panel to hear a sample of the full configuration — selected voice, language, and pace combined. The preview reads a short sample question so you can hear exactly what candidates will experience.

Always preview after making changes. Small pace differences can feel significant when heard in context.

## Setting an account default

To apply a voice configuration to all new interviews automatically:

1. Navigate to **Account Settings → Interview Defaults → Voice**.
2. Select your preferred voice, language, and pace.
3. Click **Save as Default**.

Individual interviews can override the account default during setup without affecting the global setting. The account default only applies to newly created interviews — it does not retroactively change existing sessions.
