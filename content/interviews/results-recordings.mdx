AceInt records the audio of every AI interview session by default. Recordings let you hear exactly how a candidate sounded — their confidence, pacing, and nuance — beyond what a transcript alone conveys. Every recording is synchronized to the transcript so you can click any line of text and jump directly to that moment in the audio.

## Where to find recordings

Access recordings from the session detail view:

1. Navigate to **Interviews** in the left navigation.
2. Click the completed session you want to review.
3. Select the **Recording** tab in the session detail panel.

The recording is available as soon as processing completes (typically within 5 to 10 minutes of session end). If the recording is still processing, the tab shows a **Processing** status with an estimated completion time.

## In-browser playback with transcript sync

The built-in audio player loads the recording directly in your browser — no download required. The player includes:

- **Play / Pause / Seek** controls.
- **Playback speed** — 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, 2×.
- **Volume control**.
- **Transcript sync panel** — displayed alongside the player. As the audio plays, the current turn in the transcript is highlighted. Click any turn in the transcript panel to jump the audio to that point.

Use transcript sync when reviewing a specific question's answer — click the question turn in the transcript to jump directly to that moment rather than scrubbing through the full recording.

<Callout title="Tip">
Use 1.25× or 1.5× playback speed for routine review. Switch to 1× when assessing communication clarity, confidence, or tone — nuances in delivery are easier to hear at normal speed.
</Callout>

## Downloading recordings

Download the recording in two audio formats:

<CodeTabs>
  <CodeTab label="MP3">

A compressed audio file suitable for sharing, archiving, or attaching to a candidate record in your ATS. Smaller file size.

Click **Download → MP3** in the recording player header.

  </CodeTab>
  <CodeTab label="WAV">

An uncompressed audio file with higher fidelity. Use WAV if you need to run additional audio analysis or maintain an archival-quality record.

Click **Download → WAV** in the recording player header.

  </CodeTab>
</CodeTabs>

<Callout title="Note">
Downloaded files are named automatically using the format `[role]-[session-id]-[date].mp3` (or `.wav`). Rename the file before attaching it to a candidate record to keep your ATS organized.
</Callout>

## Sharing a recording link

Share a time-limited link to the in-browser recording player with a colleague or hiring manager:

1. Open the **Recording** tab.
2. Click **Share Recording**.
3. Copy the generated link or enter an email address to send it directly from AceInt.
4. Set an expiry for the link — options are 7 days, 30 days, or custom.

Recipients access the recording in a read-only player view. They can play, pause, and use transcript sync, but cannot download the file or access any other part of your AceInt account.

## Retention window

AceInt retains recordings for **90 days** from the session date by default. After 90 days, the recording is permanently deleted. The transcript is retained separately for 365 days.

<Callout title="Warning">
Download recordings before the 90-day window closes if you need them for longer-term candidate records, compliance documentation, or audit purposes. AceInt cannot recover deleted recordings.
</Callout>

To extend the default retention window, navigate to **Account Settings → Data & Privacy → Recording Retention** and set a custom retention period (up to 365 days).

## Disabling recording for privacy-sensitive roles

For roles where recording candidates is legally restricted or where your organization's privacy policy prohibits it, you can disable recording on a per-interview basis:

1. Open the interview setup wizard or the settings of an existing interview.
2. Navigate to **Interview Settings → Recording**.
3. Toggle **Record Session** to **Off**.

When recording is disabled:

- No audio file is stored at any point during or after the session.
- Scoring is based on the transcript alone (transcription still occurs in real time during the session).
- The **Recording** tab is hidden in the session detail view.

<Callout title="Note">
Disabling recording does not affect transcription or scoring. Scores may have slightly lower confidence indicators for sessions without a recording, since the audio verification step in post-processing is skipped.
</Callout>
