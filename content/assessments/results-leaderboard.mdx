# Candidate Leaderboard

The leaderboard provides a real-time ranking of every candidate who has completed an assessment.

As new submissions are graded, the leaderboard updates automatically, allowing your hiring team to compare candidates without manually refreshing or exporting data.

Use the leaderboard to:

- Identify top-performing candidates
- Compare assessment scores
- Review completion times
- Spot anti-cheat alerts
- Collaborate with your hiring team

---

## Accessing the Leaderboard

To open the leaderboard:

1. Navigate to **Assessments**.
2. Select the assessment.
3. Open the **Leaderboard** tab.

The leaderboard is available to users with **Reviewer** access or higher.

Candidates cannot view:

- Their ranking
- Other candidates' scores
- The leaderboard itself

---

## Leaderboard Columns

By default, the leaderboard displays the following information.

| Column | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Rank** | Candidate ranking based on overall score. Ties are broken by the fastest completion time. |
| **Name** | Candidate's full name. Click the name to open the detailed score report. |
| **Score** | Overall assessment score (0–100) with an optional Pass/Fail badge. |
| **Time Taken** | Total assessment duration shown in minutes and seconds. |
| **Date Submitted** | Submission date and time based on your organization's timezone. |
| **Anti-Cheat** | Displays a warning indicator when anti-cheat events have been detected. |

Use the **Columns** menu to show or hide additional information such as:

- Question scores
- AI grading dimensions
- Custom fields

---

## Filtering and Sorting

Use filters to quickly find specific candidates.

Available filters include:

- Score range
- Pass / Fail status
- Anti-cheat flags
- Submission date range

You can sort the leaderboard by clicking any column header.

Examples:

- Highest score
- Lowest score
- Fastest completion
- Most recent submission

Click the same column again to reverse the sort order.

> **Tip**
>
> Filter by **Pass** and then sort by **Time Taken** to identify candidates who both met the required score and completed the assessment efficiently.

---

## Sharing the Leaderboard

To generate a read-only leaderboard link:

1. Click **Share Leaderboard**.
2. Enable **Share Link**.
3. Copy the generated URL.
4. Share it with your team.

People using the shared link can view:

- Rankings
- Scores
- Completion times

They **cannot**:

- View detailed score reports
- Access anti-cheat logs
- Modify assessment data

> **Warning**
>
> Shared links do not expire automatically.
>
> To revoke access, disable **Share Link**. The URL becomes invalid immediately.

Instead of using a public link, you can also invite colleagues as **Reviewers**. Reviewers have full access to:

- Candidate reports
- Leaderboard
- Anti-cheat logs

---

## Downloading the Leaderboard

To export the leaderboard:

1. Open the leaderboard.
2. Click **Export**.
3. Select **Leaderboard CSV**.

The downloaded file contains only:

- Visible columns
- Currently filtered candidates

For example:

- If only passing candidates are displayed, only those candidates appear in the export.

---

## Full Results Export

For complete assessment data—including:

- Candidate email addresses
- Per-question scores
- AI grading dimensions
- Anti-cheat event counts
- Manual score adjustments

use the **Export Results** page instead of the leaderboard export.
